Purpose

Designed for investigation, not decoration

The final site should preserve the dossier’s chronology, cross-reference every narrative claim to evidence, and make the cumulative progression across the three phases easy to inspect on screen.

  • Chronological honesty
  • Evidence-first navigation
  • Accessible reading order
  • Clear separation of report and appendix material

Phase framing

Three connected phases

Report structure

Section architecture carried over from the dossier

0. Introduction

Purpose, scope, organisation, core themes, statement of intent, and master timeline.

1. MS Teams Evidence

Operational case studies, timeline entries T-01 to T-29, and linked screenshots.

2. Systemic & Cultural Evidence

Policy contradiction, process constraint, hostility indicators, and operational context.

3. Complaints Training Session

Training content, rapid-closure concerns, ADR/deadlock analysis, and operational impact.

4. Interim Arrangements

Availability problems, weekend escalation, pay threats, and unsafe interim structure.

5. Grievance Handling

HR/ER contact, acknowledgement gaps, silence periods, and escalation to senior management.

6. Conclusions & Outcomes

Cumulative impact, organisational failure analysis, and remedies sought.

Timeline scaffold

Horizontal phase timeline placeholder

This framework already carries the top-level timeline entries from Phases.pdf. The final implementation can replace placeholder evidence URLs with deep links to exhibit pages, images, transcripts, or filtered appendix views.

Prototype notes: static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript only; suitable as a lightweight starting point for GitHub Pages or later migration into a component-based front end.
